                                PRESS RELEASE

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in the
indices to be effective from February 2, 2015 (close of January 30, 2015) on account of
proposed scheme of arrangements for amalgamation of ING Vysya Bank Ltd. with Kotak
Mahindra Bank Ltd. and Gujarat Gas Company Ltd. with GSPC Distribution Networks Ltd.
