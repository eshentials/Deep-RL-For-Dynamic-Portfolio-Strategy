PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, October 28, 2026



          Exclusion of Triveni Power Transmission Ltd. from Nifty Indices
      NSE Indices Limited vide its press release dated July 17, 2026 had announced inclusion of Triveni
      Power Transmission Ltd. (TRIVENIPT), with dummy symbol “DUMMYTRVN” on account of
      demerger of power transmission business from Triveni Engineering & Industries Ltd. in various indices
      effective from July 22, 2026 (close of July 21, 2026). After the demerger, Triveni Power Transmission
      Ltd. was listed on October 12, 2026 at the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. (NSE).

      In accordance with the index methodology, as Triveni Power Transmission Ltd. has not hit the lower
      price band on two consecutive trading days on October 27, 2026 and October 28, 2026 at NSE, the
      Index Maintenance Sub-Committee (Equity) of NSE Indices Ltd. has decided to exclude the company
      from various indices as listed hereunder effective from October 30, 2026 (close of October 29, 2026).

      It may be noted that if Triveni Power Transmission Ltd. hits the lower price band on October 29, 2026,
      the exclusion shall not be deferred further.

        Sr. No.          Index Name
           1      Nifty Microcap 250
           2      Nifty Smallcap 500
           3      Nifty Sugar & Ethanol
           4      Nifty Total Market

      For detailed guidelines regarding handling of corporate actions involving demergers in Nifty indices,
      please refer index methodology document published on our website www.niftyindices.com.
