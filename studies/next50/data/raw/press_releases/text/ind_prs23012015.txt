                                 PRESS RELEASE

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following change in the
indices on account of proposed scheme of demerger and transfer of real estate undertaking
of Arvind Ltd. into Arvind Infrastructure Ltd.

The changes in CNX 200, CNX 500, CNX Midcap and CNX Alpha index shall be effective
from February 2, 2015 (close of January 30, 2015) and change in Nifty Midcap 50 index
shall be effective from February 23, 2015 (close of February 20, 2015).
