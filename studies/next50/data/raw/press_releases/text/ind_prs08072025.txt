PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, July 08, 2025


                   Exclusion of Raymond Realty Ltd. from Nifty indices
      NSE Indices Limited vide its press release dated May 09, 2025 had announced inclusion of Raymond
      Realty Ltd. with a dummy symbol “DUMMYRAYMN” on account of demerger of company’s Real
      Estate business from Raymond Ltd. (RAYMOND) in various indices effective from May 14, 2025
      (close of May 13, 2025). After the demerger, Raymond Realty Ltd. (RAYMONDREL) was listed on
      July 01, 2025 at the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. (NSE).

      In accordance with the index methodology, as Raymond Realty Ltd. (RAYMONDREL) has not hit price
      band on two consecutive trading days on July 07, 2025 and July 08, 2025 at NSE, the Index Maintenance
      Sub-Committee (Equity) of NSE Indices Ltd. has decided to exclude Raymond Realty Ltd.
      (RAYMONDREL) from various indices as listed hereunder effective from July 10, 2025 (close of July
      09, 2025).

      It may be noted that if Raymond Realty Ltd. (RAYMONDREL) hits the price band on July 09, 2025,
      the exclusion shall not be deferred further.
