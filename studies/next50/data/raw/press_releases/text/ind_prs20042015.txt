                                  PRESS RELEASE

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the changes to the
composition and calculation methodology of CNX IT index as given hereunder:

CNX IT index is presently calculated based on the free-float market capitalization
method comprising of 20 stocks. Effective May 29, 2015 (close of May 28, 2015), the
index shall be computed with 10 companies and weights of each company in the index
shall be capped at 25% at the time of rebalancing of the index.
