PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, August 04, 2026

                             Exclusion from Nifty Waves index

      NSE vide its circular NSE/CML/75539 dated August 03, 2026 has announced suspension of trading in
      equity shares of Sab Events & Governance Now Media Ltd. with effect from August 05, 2026 on
      account of capital reduction pursuant to NCLT Order.

      In accordance with the circular, the Index Maintenance Sub-Committee (Equity) of NSE Indices
      Limited has decided to make the following exclusion from Nifty Waves index. The change shall
      become effective from August 05, 2026 (close of August 04, 2026).

      The following company is being excluded:

       Sr. No.                    Company Name                                 Symbol
          1      Sab Events & Governance Now Media Ltd.                    SABEVENTS

      No inclusion is being made in the index.
