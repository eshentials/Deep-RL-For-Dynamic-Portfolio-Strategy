The capping factor of stocks in capped indices (wherever applicable), will be
realigned to the maximum capping limit at the time of rebalancing of the index and on
a quarterly basis. Thereafter, a security's weight may go up from maximum capping
limit until next rebalancing.

The capping factor shall be calculated considering the closing prices of index
constituents 5 trading days prior to the effective date of the change.
