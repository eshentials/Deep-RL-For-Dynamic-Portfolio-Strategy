                                  PRESS RELEASE

The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following change in
CNX 500 index on account of proposed scheme of arrangement for demerger/ transfer of
various business undertakings of Jindal Stainless Ltd. into Jindal Stainless (Hisar) Ltd.,
Jindal United Steel Ltd. and Jindal Coke Ltd.

The change will be effective from June 15, 2015 (close of June 12, 2015).
