PRESS RELEASE
Mumbai, November 13, 2025


       Exclusion of Tata Motors Ltd. (formerly known as TML Commercial
                         Vehicles Ltd.) from Nifty indices

     NSE Indices Limited vide its press release dated October 07, 2025 had announced inclusion of Tata
     Motors Ltd. (formerly known as TML Commercial Vehicles Ltd.) with a dummy symbol
     “DUMMYTATAM” on account of demerger of company’s commercial vehicles business from Tata
     Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd.’s (formerly known as Tata Motors Ltd.) in various indices effective
     from October 14, 2025 (close of October 13, 2025). After the demerger, Tata Motors Ltd. (TMCV)
     was listed on November 12, 2025 at the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. (NSE).

     In accordance with the index methodology, as TMCV has not hit the price band on two consecutive
     trading days on November 12, 2025 and November 13, 2025 at NSE, the Index Maintenance Sub-
     Committee (Equity) of NSE Indices Ltd. has decided to exclude TMCV from various indices as listed
     hereunder effective from November 17, 2025 (close of November 14, 2025).

     It may be noted that if TMCV hits the price band on November 14, 2025, the exclusion shall not be
     deferred further.

About NSE Indices Limited:
NSE Indices Limited (formerly known as India Index Services & Products Ltd. - IISL), a subsidiary of
NSE, provides a variety of indices and index related services for the capital markets. The company
focuses on the index as a core product. The company owns and manages a portfolio of indices under
the Nifty brand of NSE, including the flagship index, the Nifty 50. Nifty equity indices comprises
broad-based benchmark indices, sectoral indices, strategy indices, thematic indices, and customised
indices. NSE Indices Limited also maintains fixed income indices based on Government of India
securities, corporate bonds, money market instruments and hybrid indices. Many investment products
based on Nifty indices have been developed within India and abroad. These include index-based
derivatives traded on NSE and NSE International Exchange IFSC Limited (NSE IX) and a number of
index funds and exchange traded funds. The flagship 'Nifty 50' index is widely tracked and traded as
the benchmark for Indian Capital Markets.
