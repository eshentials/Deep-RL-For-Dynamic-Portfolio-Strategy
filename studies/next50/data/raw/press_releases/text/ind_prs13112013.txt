                                    PRESS RELEASE


This is with reference to the press release issued by IISL on November 07, 2013
regarding changes in various indices effective November 15, 2013.

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No.
NSE/CMTR/24977) on November 13, 2013 regarding change in the trading holiday. As
per the circular, trading holiday on November 14, 2013 is revoked and November 15,
2013 is declared as a holiday.

Pursuant to the change in trading holiday, the proposed changes in the indices shall be
effective November 18, 2013 instead of November 15, 2013.

The details of the changes are provided in annexure 1.
