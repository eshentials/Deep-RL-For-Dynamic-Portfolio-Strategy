Securities of Marico Ltd. will be traded on ex-entitlement basis on National Stock Exchange
with effect from November 01, 2013 on account of Scheme of Arrangement between Marico
Ltd. (Demerged Company) and Marico Kaya Enterprises Limited. (Resulting Company)

various indices in this regard which will become effective from November 01, 2013.
