The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee has decided to make the following changes in
various indices to be effective from November 15, 2013 on account of:

1) Proposed voluntary delisting of Fresenius Kabi Oncology Limited; and
2) Proposed scheme of arrangement for demerger of Welspun Corp Limited

1) CNX 200 Index
